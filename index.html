About Us

Better systems. Stronger businesses.

MKBConsulting helps small and medium-sized businesses get more from their systems, processes and technology.

We work with businesses to understand how their operations function, identify inefficiencies and disconnected systems, and develop practical solutions that improve the way they work.

Our approach combines operational experience with technical systems knowledge. We focus on understanding the problem before recommending a solution, helping businesses make informed decisions about their systems without unnecessary complexity or expense.

What We Do

End-to-end support for your business systems.

Audit & Assess

We review your existing systems, processes and workflows to identify what's working, what's holding your business back, and where improvements can make the biggest difference.

Design & Plan

We turn our findings into practical recommendations, comparing options and outlining the requirements, costs, risks and implementation steps so you can move forward with confidence.

Implement & Improve

We help turn recommendations into reality, coordinating implementation, working with developers and technology providers where needed, and helping ensure the finished solution meets your business requirements.

Why Work With Us

Experience That Understands the Bigger Picture

MKBConsulting brings together experience in business operations, systems integration, technical support, CRM administration, reporting and process improvement.

That's why we focus on understanding your business first, asking the right questions and providing clear, independent advice.
